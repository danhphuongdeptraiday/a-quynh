Raw Stone & Charcoal

Level One

T1: The Ancestral Hearth

The first floor pays homage to the 'Kitchen Heart' of Hmong homes. Designed with heavy textures of charcoal slate and smoke-stained timber, this room offers a grounded atmosphere for intimate gatherings of up to six guests.

CAPACITY 2–6 Guests
VIBE Grounded & Intimate
Level Two

T2: The Indigo Loom

Elevated and airy, the second floor draws inspiration from traditional textile weaving. Subtle patterns of indigo and geometric hemp motifs are woven into the wall panels, creating a refined backdrop for celebratory dinners.

The Pinnacle

T3: The Cloud Sanctuary

The Grand Suite

Our premier suite, T3 offers complete seclusion with a private terrace and an open-fire lounge area. It is the ultimate expression of A Quỳnh's private dining concept—unrivaled, quiet, and deeply sophisticated.
